About this map

Each commune is colored by what share of a chosen income would be consumed by an estimated rent. Hover any commune for the underlying figures. Use the panel to change income, apartment size, or jump to a wage tier.

Data provenance

Measured (34 communes, hatch-free fill). Average advertised rent per m², from the Observatoire de l'Habitat / data.public.lu, twelve months ending Q4 2025. Source: published rental statistics, where the commune cleared a thirty-listing minimum.

Estimated (23 communes, hatched fill). Where rental data is suppressed but sale prices are published, rent is estimated by applying the median gross rental yield (4.4 percent) observed in the 31 communes that publish both. Read these as approximations, not measurements.

No data (43 communes, grey). Mostly small rural communes where neither rentals nor sales clear the publication threshold. Showing them grey is the honest call: a regression model could fill them in but would invent precision the source data does not have.

Method notes

The thirty-percent benchmark is the standard housing-economics affordability ceiling, not Luxembourg legal definition. Rent estimate equals commune rent per m² times selected size. Net income presets assume Class 1 single-no-dependants tax status; couples filing jointly net materially more. Advertised rents settle three to six percent below asking on average.
